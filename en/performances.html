Performances

Works spanning different moments in the history of Balé Popular do Recife and revealing the continuity of its stage research.

STAGE MEMORY · CREATION · CONTINUITY

A repertoire built across generations.

Since 1977, Balé Popular do Recife has transformed research and artistic experience into works that span different moments of its history. Official titles remain in Portuguese as part of the identity of each creation.

1977 · ORIGIN

Brincadeiras de um Circo em Decadência — Circo da Onça Malhada

The inaugural production that marked the origin of the company.

1979 · REPERTOIRE MEMORY

Prosopopéia — O Auto do Guerreiro

An early major creation bringing popular traditions and dances into the company's stage research.

1987 · 10 YEARS

Nordeste — A Dança do Brasil

A landmark work created during the company's tenth-anniversary cycle.

1992 · 15 YEARS · RESTAGING

Brasílica — O Romance da Nau Catarineta

A commemorative restaging presented during the company's fifteenth anniversary.

2007 · 30 YEARS

As Andanças do Divino

A work from the thirtieth-anniversary cycle, bringing together memory, celebration and the company's stage language.

2017 · 40 YEARS

Despertar Celebração

Created to celebrate four decades of history, bringing memory, continuity and new generations to the stage.

REPERTOIRE

Brincadeiras de Rodas, Histórias e Canções de Ninar

An encounter with games, circles and dances connecting different generations with popular culture.

PROJECT IN DEVELOPMENT

O Ritmo da Criação

A new creative universe connecting music, movement and artistic research. Its musical production is already part of the creative process, while the stage work remains in development and has not yet premiered.
