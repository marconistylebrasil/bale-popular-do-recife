BALÉ POPULAR DO RECIFE ARCHIVE

STAGE MEMORY · CREATION · CONTINUITY

A repertoire built across generations.

Since 1977, Balé Popular do Recife has transformed research and artistic experience into works spanning different moments of its trajectory. This selection brings together historical creations, restagings, anniversary productions and works that remain alive in the company's repertoire.

1977 · ORIGIN

Brincadeiras de um Circo em Decadência — Circo da Onça Malhada

The inaugural production that marked the origin of the group and the beginning of the journey that would give form to Balé Popular do Recife.

1979 · REPERTOIRE MEMORY

Prosopopéia — O Auto do Guerreiro

One of the company's first major creations, bringing popular festivities and dances into a stage construction connected to its ongoing research.

REPERTOIRE MEMORY

Oh! Linda Olinda

A production created in tribute to the city of Olinda and part of the artistic memory of Balé Popular do Recife.

1987 · 10 YEARS

Nordeste — A Dança do Brasil

A landmark work in the repertoire, created during the company's tenth-anniversary cycle and shaped by the strength of its research into Brazilian popular dance traditions.

1991 · BALÉ BRASÍLICA

O Baile do Menino Deus

A production connected to Balé Brasílica, an offshoot of Balé Popular do Recife, bringing the Northeastern Brazilian Christmas universe into the company's research and stage language.

MEMORY · CHILDREN'S PRODUCTION

As Presepadas do Dr. Munganga

A children's creation associated with André Madureira's artistic trajectory and the company's stage memory.

1992 · 15 YEARS · RESTAGING

Brasílica — O Romance da Nau Catarineta

A restaging presented during the fifteenth-anniversary cycle, bringing an existing work into the celebration of the company's artistic trajectory.

2007 · 30 YEARS

As Andanças do Divino

A work from the thirtieth-anniversary cycle, connecting memory, celebration and the stage language built by Balé Popular do Recife.

2017 · 40 YEARS

Despertar Celebração

Created to celebrate four decades of history, bringing memory, continuity and new generations together on stage.

REPERTOIRE

Brincadeiras de Rodas, Histórias e Canções de Ninar

An encounter with games, circles and dances that bring different generations closer to the universe of popular culture.

MEMORY · BALÉ BRASIL BRINQUEDO

Arlequim

A work in the trajectory of Balé Brasil Brinquedo, the children's group connected to Balé Popular do Recife, in dialogue with the universe of Brazilian popular festivities and traditions.

PROJECT IN DEVELOPMENT

O Ritmo da Criação

A new creative universe from Balé Popular do Recife, connecting music, movement and artistic research. Its musical production is already part of this creative process, while the stage production remains in development and has not yet premiered.
