ORIGIN · 1976—1977

A language born from research.

The journey that led to Balé Popular do Recife began in 1976 with André Madureira's research into Brazilian popular cultural traditions. In May 1977, that investigation took stage form with the birth of the company in Recife.

As visionary, researcher, choreographer and creator, André structured the artistic language, repertoire and training principles that would define the company's identity. His work became the foundation of the Brasílica Method and of an artistic legacy transmitted across generations.

MEMORY · CREATION · CONTINUITY

From Recife to national and international stages, a history shaped by research, artists, audiences and generations.
