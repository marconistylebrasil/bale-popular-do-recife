LIVING MEMORY · 1977—2027

Archive

Documents, images and records that preserve nearly five decades of research, creation, touring and transmission.

PRESERVE · DOCUMENT · TRANSMIT

Memory as a living part of creation.

The archive brings together historical photographs, programs and graphic materials, documents, audiovisual memory, costumes and objects connected to the trajectory of Balé Popular do Recife.

Preserving this material means keeping accessible the marks left by artists, researchers, dancers, teachers, technicians and collaborators who built the company across generations.
