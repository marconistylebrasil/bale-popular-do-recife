LIVING MEMORY · 1977—2027

Archive

A history preserved in motion. Photographs, documents, programs, videos, costumes, posters and memories span nearly five decades of creation.

A HERITAGE STILL BEING BUILT

To preserve is also to keep dancing.

The archive of Balé Popular do Recife accompanies the history of the company itself. Its records document creative processes, performances, research, travels, encounters, training and the many generations that have crossed this journey. More than testimonies of the past, these materials help us understand how an artistic language was built and remains alive.

1976—1977 · A HISTORY IS BORN

The origins recorded by the press.

Even before its premiere, the research that would give rise to Balé Popular do Recife was already appearing in newspapers. The institutional archive preserves records from Diário de Pernambuco from 1976 and May 1977, documents accompanying the transition from Grupo Circense de Dança Popular to Balé Popular do Recife.

1976 · RESEARCH

The work begins before the stage

Research led by André Madureira observed and documented traditions such as bumba-meu-boi, caboclinhos, maracatu and pastoril. This process lies at the origin of the language the company would develop.

Historical reference: Fundação Joaquim Nabuco · BPR Archive
MAY 17, 1977 · PRESS

On the eve of the premiere

The company's institutional portfolio preserves a reproduction of a Diário de Pernambuco record dated May 17, 1977, three days before the premiere at Teatro do Parque.

Document identified in the BPR 2025 Institutional Portfolio
MAY 20, 1977 · TEATRO DO PARQUE

The first stage

On May 20, 1977, the work reached the stage of Teatro do Parque. The premiere made public an investigation begun the previous year and marks the foundation of Balé Popular do Recife.

References: Prefeitura do Recife · Fundaj · Revista Continente
MAY 22, 1977 · PRESS

The first records of the journey

Another clipping preserved by the company, dated May 22, 1977, documents the first days of this history. Together, these newspapers form an essential press archive for understanding the company's birth.

Document identified in the BPR 2025 Institutional Portfolio

1977 · ORIGIN

Teatro do Parque and Circo da Onça Malhada

On May 20, 1977, the group premiered at Teatro do Parque with Brincadeiras de um Circo em Decadência. The success of the work also led to performances at Circo da Onça Malhada, on Rua da Aurora.

Research references: Fundaj · Prefeitura do Recife · Revista Continente
1987 · SOUND AND MEMORY

Nordeste — A Dança do Brasil

For the tenth anniversary, the production brought together dance, music and a new chapter in the company's artistic memory. The musical repertoire from this period also remains documented in sound recordings.
