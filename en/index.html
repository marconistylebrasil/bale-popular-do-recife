1977 — 2027

Founded in Recife on May 20, 1977, Balé Popular do Recife has built a trajectory dedicated to research, creation, training and the promotion of Brazilian popular dance traditions. Its history spans generations, national and international stages, public recognition and its own methodology for creation and teaching.

THE ORIGIN OF A LANGUAGE

CONCEPTION · RESEARCH · CREATION · METHOD

André Madureira
the visionary.

The history of Balé Popular do Recife begins with André Madureira's vision and research. As its visionary, researcher, choreographer and creator, he structured the artistic language, repertoire and training principles that defined the company's identity.

From research into Brazilian popular cultural traditions to the systematization of movement, the creation of performances and the development of the Brasílica Method, his work has crossed generations and remains the foundation of the company.

RESEARCH · CREATION · TRAINING · TRANSMISSION

Brasílica Method

Developed by André Madureira from Balé Popular do Recife's research into popular cultural traditions, Brasílica transforms investigation, documentation and artistic experience into a distinctive language of dance, creation and training.

A living heritage of the company, built through the relationship between tradition, movement, musicality, interpretation and stage presence — preserved and transmitted across generations.
