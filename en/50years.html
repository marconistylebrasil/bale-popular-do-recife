1977—2027 · TOWARDS THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY

Fifty years cannot fit into a single scene.

In May 2027, Balé Popular do Recife turns fifty. A journey that began with André Madureira's research and creation and was built by generations of artists, researchers, dancers, educators, technicians and collaborators.

The fiftieth anniversary celebrates what was created, preserves what remains and opens space for what is still to come.
