Brasílica
Method

A distinctive artistic and educational language developed from the research of André Madureira and Balé Popular do Recife.

A LIVING HERITAGE

Research transformed into language.

From the 1970s onward, André Madureira investigated Brazilian popular cultural traditions in dialogue with manifestations, masters and practitioners. Research, documentation and artistic experience became the basis for a distinctive language of movement, musicality, interpretation, stage presence and creation.

Brasílica is more than a set of steps. It is memory, research, language and transmission — a living heritage of Balé Popular do Recife, preserved and passed on across generations.

ANDRÉ MADUREIRA · CONCEPTION · RESEARCH · CREATION

A creation that remains in motion.

The research and creation of André Madureira remain the foundation of the artistic language of Balé Popular do Recife. Technical procedures, exercises, sequences and internal training materials are preserved by the company.
